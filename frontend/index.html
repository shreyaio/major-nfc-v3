Medicine pack check

To check a pack, tap it against your phone. The chip in the pack writes its own code into the link, so no app is needed — on Android or on iPhone.

Manual entry is for testing only. A code typed in by hand carries none of the protection a tap does: the whole point of tapping is that the chip supplies a value that changes every time and cannot be copied from a photograph or a screenshot. A manually entered code can be copied by anyone who has seen the pack once.

Transparency log — a daily signed record of every entry in the register, so you do not have to take our word for what it contained.
